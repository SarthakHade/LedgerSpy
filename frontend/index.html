What is LedgerSpy?

LedgerSpy is a forensic audit tool for company ledgers, intended for internal auditors, fraud investigators and compliance teams. It runs entirely on this machine: the page talks only to the local LedgerSpy service started with ledgerspy serve, which stores evidence, runs every analysis and returns the results this page displays.

What this version actually does

📂
Evidence-preserving import — working
CSV/XLSX files are hashed and kept unchanged; you confirm how columns map to the canonical schema.
🧹
Data integrity — working
Missing values, duplicates, format and balance checks with a readiness score; fixes are proposed, logged and reversible.
📏
Forensic rules + Benford — working
Ten explainable rules (duplicates, splitting, timing, dormancy, velocity…) and Benford tests with minimum-sample gating.
📈
Statistical anomalies — working
Isolation Forest, peer-group robust z-scores and tail percentiles. "Unusual" is never "fraudulent".
🧮
Review-risk score — working
A 0–100 review priority with an exact breakdown by signal. Not a probability of fraud: nothing is calibrated against confirmed outcomes yet.
🕸️
Graph and Bank Recon — Benchmarks not yet
Entity resolution, the relationship graph and bank reconciliation (CSV, XLSX and PDF statements with local OCR and review) run in the local service. Monte Carlo and benchmarking are on the roadmap and show "Not available" until built.

The scenario LedgerSpy is built for

📌 From the original problem statement (fictional scenario)

Piyush, a young Chartered Accountant, starts a high-stakes audit with a warning from the senior partner: one data leak could cost the firm its licence. With Wi-Fi disabled, he runs LedgerSpy on his laptop against the client's ledger while his teammates scroll through thousands of Excel rows — and uncovers a circular trading network of ghost vendors that had been draining funds for three years.

That scenario is the target. The roadmap in docs/roadmap.md tracks what is real and what is still to be built.

Not available yet. When supplied (roadmap P18), LedgerSpy will run thousands of simulated 12-month futures and show the survival probability with percentile bands. No projection is shown until then.
